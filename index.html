What am I looking at?

        

          A RP2350 microcontroller emulated by rp2350js in your browser,
          running a small testing (RISC-V) firmware.
        

        
    
          
  • PIO0 SM0 counts down and shows the 8-bit value on GPIO2..9.
    • 
          
  • CPU core1 outputs another counter (counting up) on GPIO16..23.
    • 
          
  • CPU core0 prints status lines to the serial, shown in the bottom window.
    • 
        

        
What can I do?

        
    
          
  • ⏪/⏩ buttons move time forward and backward. Hold to auto-repeat.
    • 
          
  • The slider scrubs the timeline (it ends once the firmware stops outputting to the serial).
    • 
          
  • The GPIO24 button resets the PIO counter and switches the core1 counter to decrement mode. Note how on click, its future consequences become visible immediately.
    • 
          
  • Click anywhere in the analyzer window to get a copyable textual signal description.
    • 
        

        
What's the point?

        
This is a simple showcase of rp2350js as well as a demonstration of a simple debugging environment with time rewind functionality.

        
It is not intended for normal use as is; instead, modify it for your specific project needs.
